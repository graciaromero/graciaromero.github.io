LEGENDARY

KnowLEdGE creation and iNcreasing acreage of legumes in Diversified cropping systems by quAntification of theiR ecosYstem services

  • Horizon Europe (Cluster 6, RIA)
  • 2024 – 2028
  • 21 partners, 11 countries

Aims to remove the barriers that hamper a significant and stable increase of the legume growing area in Europe, by quantifying the ecosystem services of annual and perennial legumes across agro-climatic zones and turning them into indicators and a decision support system for farmers.

My role: UAV and sensor-based monitoring of the legume field experiments at IRTA, and translation of the sensor data into crop and ecosystem-service traits using statistical and machine-learning models.

  • Project website

ROOT2RES

Root phenotyping and genetic improvement for rotational crops resilient to environmental change

  • Horizon Europe · Grant agreement 101060124
  • 2022 – 2027
  • 22 partners from Europe and Africa · Coordinated by ARVALIS

Develops phenotyping, genetic and modelling tools to breed cereals, potatoes and legumes with root traits adapted to future climates, making rotational cropping systems more resilient to environmental change.

My role: UAV phenotyping of above-ground traits in the IRTA field trials, complementing the root trait evaluations.
